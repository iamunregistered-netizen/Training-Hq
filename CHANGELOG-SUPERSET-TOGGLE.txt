Training HQ — Superset toggle behaviour

- The Program editor's Superset button now toggles the link on and off.
- When an exercise is already linked, selecting “− Remove Superset” clears the shared superset group from both linked exercise cards. Both exercises remain in the programme as independent exercises.
- Removing either exercise window also clears the superset group from its partner, so the remaining exercise becomes independent rather than retaining a broken link.
- Both linked cards display the active pair colour and numbered label; after unlinking, the colour and label are removed.
- The action is available from either member of the pair.
- Existing exercise prescriptions, ramp rows, and other programme data are preserved.
