TRAINING HQ — BACKUP & SYNC UPDATE

1. Export a JSON backup from your current Training HQ before deployment.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Replace the matching files in your existing Cloudflare project with the files in this package. Keep your existing Cloudflare account, Worker name and OAuth client configuration.
4. Deploy the Worker.
5. Open Training HQ > Settings > Backup & sync.
6. Confirm your existing Google OAuth Web Client ID is present. If not, enter it.
7. Select Connect Google Drive and approve the requested Drive access. A first backup is uploaded immediately.
8. Leave Automatic backup enabled. Changes are uploaded about two minutes after the last save while the app is open.
9. Test Back up now, then check Google Drive for Training HQ Backup.json and a dated daily snapshot.

RESTORE
- Select Restore from Drive. The app downloads a safety JSON export first, asks for confirmation, validates the Drive backup, then replaces local data.
- Do not clear browser storage during deployment.

LIMITATIONS
- Browser OAuth tokens are not stored permanently. Google may ask you to reconnect after closing/reopening the app.
- The browser cannot back up while the app is fully closed. This update is not a server-side scheduled backup.
- Daily snapshots currently accumulate; delete older snapshots manually in Google Drive if desired.
