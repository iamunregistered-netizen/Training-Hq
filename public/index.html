Settings

Your maxes (1RM)

Add any lift or exercise you want to use as a percentage-based reference. Saving a reference maximum automatically adds its exercise name to the Exercise Library.

Lifter profile

Bodyweight and age are saved on this device. Age is recorded for context; the Strength Level-style estimated 1RM calculation does not adjust for age.

Projected one-rep max

Enter a recent hard set to estimate 1RM using the average of Epley, Brzycki, Lander, Lombardi and Mayhew formulas. Most reliable for 1–10 reps close to failure.

Training cycle

Progression is configured separately for each program on the Programs page. You can turn it off, use the built-in 12-week cycle, or create custom 12-week engines.

Backup & restore

Your records stay on this device. Export a JSON backup regularly; import it to restore or move your data. CSV is available for spreadsheet analysis.

Important: browser storage can be cleared by Android or Chrome. Export backups regularly.

Cross-device sync

Keeps programs, logs and settings in step across your phone and PC. Photos stay on the device that took them.
